I’m Den Ree (Dzianis Rusakovich), a musician and live coder originally from Belarus, now based in Rotterdam.

I perform electronic music through live coding as part of A/V performances in what I call algo-DJ sets. This is a way to tell stories and process emotions around them.

I also build tools that support the creative process in everyday life, and I’m part of NDA/studio, a creative technology studio.

My background in software development and my artistic practice complement each other, shaping how I approach things that I’m creating: from product thinking, code and architecture to creative technology and musical expression.
